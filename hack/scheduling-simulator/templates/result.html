Allocation method & exclusions

For each MC and pool, we sum actual node SKU prices. Each size's weight is its largest fraction of that pool's usable CPU, memory, SWIFT NIC or pod capacity, using only real HCP pods. We normalize those weights to allocate the entire pool bill, including system reservations, daemonsets, buffers, rollout/AZ headroom, growth placeholders and idle/balancing nodes. Costs are averaged across the real HCPs of each size, weighted by identical-MC counts. Count × cost per HCP sums to the fleet VM bill (before display rounding). Sizes absent from the fleet show demand only; they have no amortized cost estimate.

This is a shared-cost allocation, not the marginal price of adding a cluster. The optimizer minimizes cores, not dollars. VM compute only: excludes disks, networking, load balancers, AKS fees, service-cluster infrastructure, licenses, support, labor and taxes. No reservations, savings plans or negotiated discounts are assumed. The two policy tables are labeled independently of the visualizer's policy selection.
